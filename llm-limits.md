* Continual Orchestration
  * real-time coordination of attention, execution, and interrupts
  * i.e. manage/orchestrate my laptop's entire day
  * interrupts, personal vs work, short vs long priorities, time mgt, long-term life mgt
  * continual: not only continuous/instantaneous/always-on, but also across the continuum of teleological considerations
  * scaffoldability: moderate
  * evaluable: moderate
  * Evals
* Judgement under Epistemic Friction
  * make high-stakes decisions when complete ground truth is unavailable
  * example frictions: delayed outcomes, ambiguous signals, hidden confounders, shifting constraints, low-frequency catastrophic failures, non-repeatable contexts, weak ground truth, multi-stakeholder disagreement, unquantifiable tail-risks, adversarial deception, timescales too short for full investigation
  * humans compensate for validation friction with heuristics based on their teleological/epistemic graphs
  * scaffoldability: hard
  * evaluable: extremely hard
  * Evals
* Continual Learning
  * LLMs unable to update epistemic graph as embodied in model weights
  * scaffold/harness solutions will be clunky/inefficient, but often legible
  * scaffoldability: straightforward but clunky
  * evaluable: straightforward
* Risk-bearing Agency
  * absorb legal, financial, and reputational consequences within a human ecosystem.
  * not trusted because not accountable
  * related to: legibility of reasoning, incentives, liability
  * a relational property: responsibility has to be given/earned

2 Conceptual Graphs

* Epistemic Graph: what you hold true
  * what you believe, know, grasp, find
  * epistemic hygiene, hallucination, PoV
* Teleological Graph: what you hold dear
  * what you value, want, intend, seek
  * directed, weighted: values/beliefs/principles/utility, desires/instincts/drives/appetites/needs (cf. Maslow), goals, intentions, plans, priorities
  * critical for identity, PoV, responsibility

2 Bottleneck Dimensions

* Structural: Epistemic vs. Teleological
* Temporal: short vs long horizons
  * short: interrupts, multitasking
  * long: values, goals, delayed validation
