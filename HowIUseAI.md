# How I Use AI

- [BrianThinks](https://holtz.org/BrianThinks): my AI think-alike bot
- [humanknowledge.net/wiki](https://humanknowledge.net/wiki/#!index.md): my research wiki
- [Purissima Water District president](https://holtz.org/PurissimaWater/PHWD.md): AI briefs me for board meetings, critiques staff work, etc.
- Santa Clara County Libertarian Party secretary/treasurer: AI generates financial reports, filings, minutes, etc.
- Medical history: AI distills all my medical records into markdown, to give health advice
- Genealogy research: AI helps audit/scrub/extend my FamilyTreemaker/ancestry.com tree
- Family logs: AI helps maintain/augment my one-line-per-day log of family history
- Family encyclopedia: everything AI should know about our household to give us advice
  - e.g. organizing NYC apartment search for my daughter's summer internship
- Tech support: AI helps set up new MacBook, reconcile GDrive contents, backups, etc.
