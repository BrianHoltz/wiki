Entity counts

Independent scoped reference counts; kinds, instances and catalogue records are not interchangeable. Counts do not roll up through the tree. ℵ₀ means countably infinite; 𝔠 = 2^ℵ₀ means uncountably infinite. No continuum-hypothesis assumption is made.

ℵ₀: countably infinite. 𝔠 = 2ℵ₀: uncountably infinite, the cardinality of the continuum; 𝔠 is not assumed to equal ℵ₁. ☠︎ follows supported extinct counts and ♥︎ follows extant counts, with extant last. Missing splits are omitted, never treated as zero.
