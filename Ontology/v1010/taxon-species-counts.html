Taxon species counts

Research retrieved 2026.10.10. Suggestive counts of identified species, independently selected for each ontology node and each extinction category from high-quality reference taxonomies. Closest reference taxa and proxies are explicit. Extinct and extant sources need not agree or add up, and counts never roll up through ontology children. No composite counts are assembled for paraphyletic groups. Omit unavailable counts; a known unsplit total has no status glyph. At most three significant digits with K/M, retaining significant trailing zeros. ☠︎ means catalogued extinct species; ♥︎ means extant or source-reported non-fossil/living-tree proxy, always last. Text presentation keeps the symbols monochrome.

Tree numbers are compact, suggestive counts: a lone number is a total without a split; ☠︎ follows extinct counts and ♥︎ follows extant counts, with extant last. Unavailable counts are omitted. Living/non-fossil checklist proxies can retain recently extinct species. Click a tree count for its reference taxon and method. Counts from independent sources need not agree or add up; source coverage and extinction periods differ.
