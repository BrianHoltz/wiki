SUMO projected hierarchy

A browseable single-parent projection of the Suggested Upper Merged Ontology.

SUMO is a formal multiply-inherited ontology, not a tree. Where the official Ontology4 PDF contains a node, this view chooses the shortest measured directed blue arc among its direct parents and drops the longer cross-links from the visible tree. Every dropped direct parent remains visible on the node as an alternate parent.

Unary nodes

These nodes have exactly one child in the projected tree. They are retained for now and listed here for structural review.
