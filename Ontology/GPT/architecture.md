# Architectural rationale

This is a replacement proposal, not an edit to the existing repository. It provides **555 canonical classes with definitions, 32 additional superclass links, 46 defined cross-cutting class expressions, and 20 typed predicate examples**. Every one of the active tree's **439 records** has an explicit coverage mapping. Mapping is not a claim that every replacement has exactly the original extension.

## The main decisions

**Entity means “that which can be referred to, even if nonexistent.”** Reference does not establish actual existence. Whether an expression such as “the largest prime number” refers to a nonexistent entity or fails to refer remains open. This root definition does not supply an identity criterion or ancestry for every nonexistent referent; the treatment of fictional referents remains a separate design question.

**Keep Entity as the root; place predicate specifications inside Abstract entity.** The useful distinction is concrete realization versus abstract identity, not “takes no arguments” versus “takes arguments.” A unary property specification and a binary relation specification can both be entities, and their particular bearers and realizations can be entities too. This avoids treating redness, one apple's color, and the value representing that color as one thing.

The root has two navigation routes, **Realized entity** and **Abstract entity**. This is a selective organizing distinction, not a declaration that all metaphysical problems have been solved. Abstract identity does not require Platonism; concrete realization does not require a universal present. Models can describe particulars through three-dimensional or spacetime representations.

**Realized entity separates organization, occurrence, extent, condition, and bearer dependence.** Its primary children are Object, Region, Process, State instance, Dependent feature, and Socially constituted entity. These are useful identity criteria, with explicit overlap permitted. Region is promoted out of Object: a room space, hole space, or jurisdictional extent is not a material structure merely because material surrounds or occupies it.

Object contains Item, Material portion, Collection, Social object, Physical field configuration, and Information token. An item has constituent unity; a portion has amount/composition identity; a collection has membership identity. These distinctions prevent an atom from being a portion of an element and a molecule from being a portion of a compound. The original origin categories remain useful, but biotic and artifact classifications may overlap. Naturalness is a separate identity/origin qualification, not a prohibition on cultivated organisms.

**Abstract entity separates structures, content, values, predicates, and units.** A text, image, algorithm, or rule is distinguished from its concrete carrier or enactment. A proposition differs from its expression, the state it describes, and a subject's belief in it. A proof differs from the proposition proved. A language system differs from an expression in that language. Mathematical objects are not grouped under fields of study.

**Kinds determine primary homes; qualifications remain explicit.** Agent, food, product, location, and instrument classifications depend on context. Their specifications are unary predicates; actual qualifying entities are described by separate class expressions. Biological diet, habitat, size, thermoregulation, and form classes likewise remain available without rearranging ancestry. Designed purpose can legitimately define an artifact kind: a weapon is identified by its construction and design purpose, whereas a rock used as a weapon has an instrumental role without becoming a designed weapon.

**Keep ancestry; compress navigation.** Selected source-backed clades preserve birds within dinosaurs, mammals within synapsids, and humans within lobe-finned vertebrates. Some canonical edges jump intervening classes to keep the longest visible path at 13 edges. Alternate parents and full retrieved source lineages preserve the omitted ancestry. For example, Human appears directly below Ape while retaining Great ape as an alternate superclass. This is subclass inclusion, never a claim that intervening clades disappear. The retrieved [NCBI human lineage](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/Taxonomy/Browser/wwwtax.cgi?id=9606) and [bird lineage](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/Taxonomy/Browser/wwwtax.cgi?id=8782) provide explicit cross-checks.

## Formal interpretation

Every tree node denotes a **class**, and each primary or alternate parent asserts subclass inclusion: if an individual instantiates the child, it instantiates the parent. None of the tree edges mean instance-of, part-of, located-in, or used-for. Those relations have separate typed predicate examples.

A node such as Property specification denotes a class whose instances are unary specifications. It does not denote the set of all ordinary things possessing a property. A food-bearing expression means: a material portion satisfies a context-bound specification that is an instance of Food specification. It does not make the portion an abstract predicate. Context-dependent unary specifications close over their interpretation; explicitly exposed context arguments make an application relation multiargument.

A formal export must keep class symbols, their reified specification objects, ordinary individuals, and predicate symbols distinct. For example, `class:Human` is a class symbol; `spec:Human` can be a separately reified kind specification associated with it. No unrestricted comprehension is assumed. A relation specification can itself have an arity or provenance without turning every assertion about it into unrestricted higher-order logic. [OWL's structural specification](https://www.w3.org/TR/owl2-syntax/) supplies useful class/individual/property distinctions, but this proposal is not a claim of an already complete OWL axiomatization.

## Remaining ambiguities

- **Upper-level overlap:** object/process, state/feature, and abstract structure/content boundaries remain interpretive. No sibling-disjointness or exhaustive-union axioms are silently inferred. Root categories remain open to future extension.
- **Social identity:** organizations have membership, charter, and recognition aspects. Organization is primarily a Social group, with Social object as an additional superclass; purely legal or automated organizations require explicit criteria. Currency systems, units, and monetary tokens are separated, but accounts and claims merit further refinement.
- **Personhood:** biological humans have a kind home; personhood is a criterion-dependent qualification admitting legal persons and possible nonhuman subjects. Sentience and cognition are capacities, not universal consequences of agent status.
- **Contextual facets:** ordinary fish, algae, slime molds, dietary carnivores, and similar classes are intentionally not clean clades. Their operational criteria need domain decisions; the machine-readable expressions identify this limitation rather than pretending a label determines an extension.
- **Biology:** 59 taxon identities and 61 superclass links were checked against retrieved NCBI records. This is a dated ancestry cross-check, not definitive nomenclatural certification. NCBI itself cautions against treating it as the final classification authority. Synapsida is an explicit fossil-inclusive editorial extension absent from the retrieved intervening lineage. Metatheria and Eutheria are labeled to distinguish stem-inclusive lineages from familiar crown-only readings. Taxonomic absence does not mean extinction; unknown extinct status remains unasserted. Viruses, viroids, and prions are biological infectious classes, not one asserted ancestry clade.
- **Coverage:** all active records are accounted for; archived alternative trees, lexical reservoirs, and the millions of entities in cited prior-art systems are not exhaustively imported. Several source definitions need corrected or broader routes rather than exact replacements.

The validation checks structure, references, source accounting, formal levels, retrieved ancestry, and selected category-mistake counterexamples. They do not prove the ontology uniquely correct or philosophically complete. The proposal's substantive content is complete for review; these ambiguities are documented design boundaries, not placeholders for omitted branches.
