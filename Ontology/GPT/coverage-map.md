# Complete original-to-proposal coverage map

All 439 original records are accounted for. This measures explicit **concept accounting**, not 439 exact synonym matches. Definitions and questionable source placements are intentionally revised.

**Revised** retains the intended core kind with reviewed wording and placement. **Disambiguated** separates conflated senses or identity criteria; some target classes are broader routes requiring a contextual facet. **Faceted** preserves a cross-cutting class expression outside the canonical hierarchy. **Merged alias** repairs duplicate spellings or typography.

Targets named as specifications classify predicates, not their ordinary bearers. `facet:` targets preserve bearer classifications. Broad destinations such as Process or Region do not claim extensional equivalence.
