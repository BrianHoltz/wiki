Wikipedia Category Browser

This browser explores a static snapshot of Wikipedia’s category system, beginning with Main topic classifications. Expand a category to browse its locally packaged subcategories. Search finds categories in the snapshot, and every category has a link to its live Wikipedia page.
Wikipedia categories are a many-parent graph, not a clean taxonomy. They include article topics, people, places, maintenance groupings, and navigation aids; cycles and duplicate paths are possible. This snapshot contains the two navigation roots through depth 3. Deeper categories remain available through their live links. This page does not claim that a category is a noun class or a 20 Questions branch.
